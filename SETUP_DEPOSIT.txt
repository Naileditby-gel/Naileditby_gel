CLIENT FLOW
- Client chooses a date/time and service.
- Client sees: ₱100 GCash deposit, +63 961 888 8216, Angel Joy.

OWNER FLOW
- Open /admin.html and log in.
- Open Online Bookings → Pending.
- Verify the GCash payment against the reference number.
- Tap Verify Deposit.

- Confirmed slots remain unavailable to other clients.

IMPORTANT
The website does not connect directly to GCash or automatically verify payments. Gel verifies the payment in the GCash app, then marks the deposit paid in the dashboard.
Never place a GCash PIN, OTP, password, or secret key in the website.
